Specifications

Measured limits, supported settings, and test conditions will replace early estimates.

Review requiredNo final electrical, throughput, baud, power, or environmental specifications are published yet.

Specification format

  • Board revision and firmware revision.
  • Supply voltage and measured current.
  • Logic voltage and input/output limits.
  • Supported UART formats and tested baud rates.
  • USB host count, OS, and descriptor behavior tested.
  • Ambient and enclosure conditions for each result.
  • Known deviations and out-of-range behavior.

Limits and capabilities

Configuration maxima and datasheet limits are not product claims. The final specification will identify only behavior verified on the named revision and test setup.
