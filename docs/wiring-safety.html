Wiring and safety

Use only the pinout and electrical limits for the exact UARTee hardware revision.

Important limitsUARTee is not an RS-232 adapter and does not provide galvanic isolation. It also uses a shared ground unless a future revision documents otherwise.

Before applying power

  • Confirm target logic voltage against the revision-specific pinout.
  • Do not connect RS-232, RS-485, industrial, or mains-level signals directly.
  • Check common-ground continuity before energizing both sides.
  • Observe the documented power-source and power-sequencing limits.
  • Use current limiting and protection for temporary or unfamiliar equipment.

Signal connections

A released board diagram will show UART A and UART B numbering, transmit and receive direction, ground, and any optional control signals.

TX → target RX
RX ← target TX
GND ↔ target GND

Do not infer direction from connector position or a previous prototype revision.
